# Seven Days of Rethinking

The 7-day email series readers receive after the Africa Lens Quiz, one email a day.

- `day-1.md` to `day-7.md`: each day in English, French, Hausa, Arabic and Kiswahili.
- Sent by MailerLite automations, one per language. The quiz passes the reader's
  language (`language` field: en, fr, ha, ar, sw) so each reader gets their own language.
- `{$name}` is MailerLite's first-name tag. Keep it exactly as written.
- Day 1 is sent one day after the quiz; each later day one day after the previous one.
- After Day 7 readers move to the weekly letter.

These files are not part of the website (they live outside `site/`).
