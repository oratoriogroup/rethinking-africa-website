## English

**Subject:** WHAT WILL YOU BUILD?
**Preview:** Africa's unfinished work is not only a tragedy to describe. It is an assignment to pick up.

Hello {$name},

For seven days, I have asked you to look again.

At the lens.

At education.

At interrupted knowledge.

At who gets recognised as an innovator.

At the stories we repeat.

At the false choice between inheritance and modernity.

But Rethinking Africa cannot end with better arguments.

Africa has enough brilliant diagnoses.

The question is what happens after we understand the problem.

Across the continent are unfinished questions.

Knowledge that needs documentation.

Industries that need transformation.

Institutions that need reform.

Stories that need retelling.

Technologies that need adaptation.

Communities whose solutions have not yet met researchers.

Researchers whose ideas have not yet met communities.

This is why I do not want your Africa Lens profile to become another personality label.

Let it become an assignment.

If you are a **Knowledge Recoverer**, what is disappearing around you that should be documented?

If you are a **Bridge Builder**, which two worlds need to meet?

If you are a **System Reformer**, which broken process have people learnt to tolerate?

If you are a **Story Restorer**, which damaging narrative can you interrupt?

If you are a **Future Maker**, what have you been waiting for permission to begin?

So here is the last question:

**Name one thing you will recover, reform, retell or build in the next 90 days.**

Reply with one sentence.

Not a proposal.

Not a five-year plan.

One sentence.

I want Rethinking Africa to become more than a place where we discuss Africa.

I want it to become a place where Africans and those committed to Africa think differently, connect knowledge, recover agency and begin things.

Because Africa must not only be studied.

Africa must speak.

Africa must think.

Africa must create.

Africa must determine.

Thank you for spending these seven days with me.

Dr Damola
