Editorial Standards

The platform sits between scholarship, journalism, documentary and public education. These standards keep it trustworthy.

Our standards

How we work

Research verification

Claims are supported by credible research, historical records, data, field observation or community testimony before publication.

Source selection

We favour primary sources, peer-reviewed work and named expertise, and clearly distinguish facts from interpretation and speculation.

Corrections

Verified errors are corrected promptly and visibly on the relevant page or episode description. Corrections may be requested at hello@rethinkingafrica.africa.

Guest representation

Guests are quoted in context, informed of how material will be used, and may review their approved photographs and quotations.

AI & dramatisation disclosure

Where artificial intelligence, reenactment or dramatisation is used, it is disclosed in the content itself.

Protection of contributors

Submitted stories are handled confidentially; nothing is published without consent, and identities are protected on request.

Citation policy

References and reading lists

Major episodes carry their references and reading lists on the Ideas page, so viewers, students and researchers can verify sources and go deeper.
